Release of Liability & Service Agreement

Version · Effective
Please read carefully. This is a legal agreement. By signing it you give up certain legal rights, including the right to sue Bluff Road Bike Co LLC for injury or damage caused by its ordinary negligence. Sections marked for initials must be initialed individually.

1. Parties & Scope

This agreement is between the undersigned customer ("I," "me") and Bluff Road Bike Co LLC, an Indiana limited liability company, together with its owner, members, employees and agents (collectively, "the Shop"). It applies to every bicycle, component and accessory I leave with the Shop for inspection, adjustment, repair, assembly, cleaning or any other service ("Service"), on the date below and on any later visit.

2. Authorization of Work

I authorize the Shop to perform the Service we discussed and to test-ride or operate the bicycle as reasonably needed to diagnose or confirm the work. Any price given is an estimate. The Shop will contact me for approval before doing additional work that would raise the total beyond the estimate. The minimum labor charge is $20.

3. Assumption of Risk

I understand that cycling is a physical activity with inherent risks, including falls, collisions, equipment failure, and serious injury or death, and that bicycles and components can fail even when properly serviced, through wear, fatigue, manufacturing defects, crashes, improper use or conditions outside the Shop's control. I knowingly and voluntarily assume all such risks, whether known or unknown.

4. Release of Liability, Including Negligence

To the fullest extent permitted by Indiana law, I release, waive and discharge the Shop from any and all claims, demands and causes of action for personal injury, death, or property damage arising from or related to the Service or my later use of the bicycle, INCLUDING CLAIMS CAUSED BY THE NEGLIGENCE OF THE SHOP. This release does not apply to the Shop's gross negligence or willful or intentional misconduct.

5. Pre-Existing Conditions & Parts

The Shop is not responsible for prior wear, damage, fatigue, corrosion or defects that exist before the Service or that are unrelated to the work performed, including issues not visible during a routine inspection. Parts installed carry only the manufacturer's warranty, if any. Customer-supplied parts are installed at my own risk.

6. Indemnification

I agree to indemnify, defend and hold the Shop harmless from any claim brought by a third party (including any other person who rides, owns or uses the bicycle after the Service) arising from the bicycle or the Service, except to the extent caused by the Shop's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7. Personal Property

Please remove lights, computers, bags, bottles and other removable accessories before drop-off. The Shop is not responsible for loss of or damage to items left with the bicycle that are not part of the Service.

8. Pickup & Unclaimed Bicycles

I agree to pick up my bicycle within a reasonable time after being notified that the Service is complete. A bicycle left more than thirty (30) days after that notice, following reasonable attempts to contact me at the phone and email below, may be considered abandoned and may be sold, donated or otherwise disposed of as permitted by Indiana law, with proceeds applied to any unpaid balance.

9. Age & Authority

I certify that I am at least eighteen (18) years old and that I own the bicycle or am authorized by its owner to leave it for Service. If the bicycle is for a rider under 18, I am that rider's parent or legal guardian and I sign this agreement on the minor's behalf and on my own behalf.

10. Electronic Signature & Records

I agree to sign this agreement electronically and to receive a copy by email. My drawn signature and typed name below are my legal signature and have the same force and effect as a handwritten signature under the federal E-SIGN Act and the Indiana Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (Ind. Code § 26-2-8).

11. Severability & Entire Agreement

If any part of this agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder stays in full force, and the invalid part will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted. This is the entire agreement regarding its subject and supersedes any prior service waiver I signed with the Shop.

12. Governing Law & Venue

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Indiana. Any dispute will be resolved exclusively in a court of competent jurisdiction located in Johnson County, Indiana.
